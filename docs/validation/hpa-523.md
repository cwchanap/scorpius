## Displaced committed-pusher evidence (automated, domain)

`displaced_controller_resolves_damage_without_its_lost_push` (`src/domain/enemy.rs`) commits a Controller push on a vertical lane over the Vanguard, shoves the Controller perpendicular off that lane with a live player push, then resolves the enemy phase:

- the locked attack still rolls against and damages the Vanguard (`AttackRolled` hit + `DamageApplied` from `IMPULSE_PROJECTOR`, exact seeded HP pinned);
- no `UnitPushed` event for the player ever occurs — the lost lane means no enemy push;
- the phase lands cleanly back in `Player` with `result()` unset — no `BattleError`, no stuck `EnemyResolution`.

Supporting planner/reach evidence: `controller_steps_onto_a_reachable_aligned_push_lane`, `controller_without_an_aligned_lane_falls_back_to_the_attack_band`, `controller_dynamic_intent_center_satisfies_weapon_reaches` (dynamic intents cannot commit an illegal push target), `forced_diagonal_push_target_is_rejected_before_commitment`. Range/push alignment goes through the shared `weapon_reaches` predicate.
